Questions

Good to know
+How long will my order take?
Every piece is made to order — printed by hand once you buy, so nothing is wasted. Production takes a few days, then shipping. Standard delivery is free and takes around 30 days worldwide; Express (~10 days) is available at checkout. You'll get a tracking number once it ships.
+Why is it made to order?
Because it's the KALMERA way — the fewest, best pieces, nothing wasted. Making each piece only when it's ordered means no overproduction, no landfill, no dead stock. The trade-off is a short wait; the upside is a piece made for you.
+What sizing should I choose?
The pieces are cut oversized / boxy by design. If you want a true oversized look, take your normal size; if you prefer a closer fit, size down. A full size guide with measurements is on each product.
+Do you ship worldwide?
Yes — worldwide. Standard shipping is free everywhere. Delivery times vary a little by country; you'll see the estimate and a tracking number after ordering.
+Returns & exchanges
As each piece is made to order, we can't accept general change-of-mind returns. But if your item arrives faulty, damaged, or the wrong item/size, email us within 7 days at kalmera.team@gmail.com with a photo and we'll put it right. Check the size guide before ordering to get the fit right first time.
+How do I get in touch?
Email kalmera.team@gmail.com for anything — orders, sizing, or just to say hello. We read every message.
